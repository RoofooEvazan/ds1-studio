DT1 Viewer

Your files stay on your computer: they are read here in the browser, nothing is uploaded.

Choose MPQ files and select d2data.mpq, d2exp.mpq and patch_d2.mpq from your Diablo II folder (Ctrl+click to pick several). Add a mod's .mpq files too, such as ProjectD2's pd2data.mpq, pd2assets.mpq and pd2maps.mpq: a mod's tiles win over the game's, as in game.

A mod's loose data folder can be added with “Choose a data folder”. Files you pick are only read here in the browser.
